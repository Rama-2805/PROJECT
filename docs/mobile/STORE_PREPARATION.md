## Privacy Policy (Summary)

SmartCampus AI Privacy Policy:

1. **Data Collection:** We collect student attendance data, face embeddings for recognition, and engagement metrics. Face images are converted to mathematical embeddings and original images are not stored beyond enrollment processing.

2. **Data Usage:** Data is used solely for attendance marking, behavior analytics, and providing AI-powered insights to authorized users (students, parents, teachers, administrators).

3. **Data Retention:** Face embeddings are retained while the student is enrolled. Attendance records are retained for 5 years for academic purposes. Analytics data is anonymized after 1 year.

4. **Data Sharing:** We do not share biometric data with third parties. Aggregated, anonymized analytics may be used for research purposes.

5. **User Rights:** Users can request data export, deletion, and access logs via the Privacy Center in the web dashboard.

6. **Security:** All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Face embeddings are stored with additional encryption.

Full policy: https://smartcampus-ai.com/privacy
