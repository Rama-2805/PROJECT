# SmartCampus AI — Brand Identity Guide

## Brand Overview

- **Product Name:** SmartCampus AI
- **Tagline:** "The Future of Intelligent Education"
- **Brand Promise:** Transform university classrooms into intelligent, data-driven learning environments using AI, computer vision, and IoT edge computing.
- **Target Audience:** University administrators, department heads, IT directors, faculty, and institutional decision-makers.

---

## Logo

### Primary Logo
```
┌─────────────────────────┐
│    ◇                     │
│   ╱ S ╲   SmartCampus    │
│  ╱     ╲     AI          │
│ ╱       ╲                │
│ ◇  ◆  ◇                 │
└─────────────────────────┘
```

The logo combines:
- **Hexagon shape** — Represents connectivity, IoT nodes, and network architecture
- **Letter "S"** — Stylized monogram for "Smart"
- **Three dots** — Represent students, teachers, and administrators connected through AI
- **Gradient** — From AI Blue (#22D3EE) to AI Purple (#A78BFA)

### Logo Variations
- **Full Logo:** Wordmark + icon (light and dark variants)
- **Icon Only:** Hexagon monogram for favicons and app icons
- **Wordmark:** Text-only for dense layouts

## Visual Style

### Glassmorphism
- Semi-transparent backgrounds with backdrop blur
- Subtle border highlights
- Gradient overlays on cards and panels

### Animations
- Smooth transitions (300ms ease-smooth)
- Fade-up on scroll
- Stagger animations for lists
- Glow pulse for live indicators

### Gradients
- Primary: `from-ai-blue to-ai-purple`
- Success: `from-emerald-400 to-cyan-400`
- Warning: `from-amber-400 to-orange-400`

## Presentation Template

### Slide Structure
1. Cover slide: Logo + Title + Tagline
2. Problem statement: Current challenges
3. Solution: SmartCampus AI overview
4. Architecture: System diagram
5. AI Pipeline: Face enrollment flow
6. Live Dashboard: Screenshots
7. IoT Network: Edge device management
8. Analytics & Insights: AI-generated reports
9. Results: Metrics and impact
10. Future Vision: Roadmap and expansion
11. Thank you: Contact and call to action

### Color Usage in Presentations
- Dark backgrounds (near-black)
- Gradient accents on key points
- White text for readability
- AI Blue highlights for calls to action
- 16:9 aspect ratio for all slides
