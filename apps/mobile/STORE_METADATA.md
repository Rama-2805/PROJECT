[English]
SmartCampus AI transforms university classrooms into intelligent, data-driven learning environments. Using advanced AI face recognition, behavioral analytics, and real-time monitoring, it provides students, parents, teachers, and administrators with actionable insights into attendance, engagement, and campus operations.

**For Students:**
• Track your daily attendance and view subject-wise reports
• Get AI-powered insights about your learning patterns
• Chat with the AI Student Assistant for instant answers
• View your class schedule and receive reminders
• Scan QR codes for emergency attendance marking
• Monitor your engagement scores and academic progress

**For Parents:**
• Receive real-time notifications when your child attends class
• View weekly and monthly attendance trends
• Get alerts about attendance drops or unusual absences
• Monitor academic progress and engagement summaries
• Communicate directly with teachers and administration

**For Teachers:**
• Start and manage live class sessions with AI attendance
• View real-time student detection and engagement metrics
• Receive AI alerts for low engagement or unusual behavior
• Override AI decisions and verify attendance manually
• Generate QR codes for backup attendance
• Access classroom analytics and student insights

**Key Features:**
• Real-time AI face recognition attendance (99.1% accuracy)
• Behavioral analytics: attention, phone usage, distraction detection
• Push notifications for attendance, alerts, and announcements
• Biometric authentication (Face ID / fingerprint)
• Offline mode with automatic sync
• Dark mode UI with premium glass-morphism design
• Secure end-to-end encrypted data transmission
• Multi-language support coming soon

**Security & Privacy:**
• All data encrypted in transit and at rest
• Biometric authentication for secure access
• GDPR and FERPA compliant

SmartCampus AI is free for students. Institutional subscriptions available for schools and universities.

SmartCampus AI is the premier AI-powered campus management platform that brings intelligence to education. Powered by advanced computer vision and machine learning, it helps educational institutions track attendance, analyze student engagement, and improve learning outcomes.

**What is SmartCampus AI?**
SmartCampus AI uses state-of-the-art face recognition and behavioral analysis to automate attendance, provide real-time classroom insights, and help educators understand student engagement patterns. The platform serves four key user groups:

**Students:**
• View your attendance percentage and subject-wise breakdown
• Receive AI insights about your learning habits

• Use QR code scanning for quick attendance

**Parents:**

• Track weekly and monthly performance trends

• View comprehensive academic analytics
• Contact teachers when needed

**Teachers:**
• Launch AI-powered attendance sessions instantly
• Monitor live classroom engagement metrics
• Receive actionable AI alerts during class
• Generate QR codes for backup attendance
• Access historical data and student insights

**Why SmartCampus AI?**
• Industry-leading 99.1% face recognition accuracy

• Works offline with automatic sync
• Enterprise-grade security and encryption
• Modern, intuitive dark mode interface
