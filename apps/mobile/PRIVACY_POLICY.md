# SmartCampus AI — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: June 14, 2026**

## Overview

SmartCampus AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of students, parents, teachers, and administrators who use our platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Information You Provide

- **Account Information**: Name, email address, role (student/parent/teacher/admin), institution affiliation
- **Profile Information**: Profile photo, department, student/employee ID, academic details
- **Communication**: Messages sent through the AI Assistant and teacher communication features

### 2. Information Collected Automatically

- **Attendance Data**: Face recognition embeddings (encrypted), attendance timestamps, classroom locations
- **Behavioral Data**: Engagement scores, attention patterns, anonymized behavior metrics
- **Device Information**: Device model, OS version, app version, device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: Screen views, feature interactions, session duration (anonymized)

### 3. Camera & Biometric Data

- **Face Recognition**: Short-lived face embeddings are captured during attendance sessions. These are encrypted at rest and automatically deleted after 30 days.
- **Biometric Authentication**: On-device biometric data (Face ID / fingerprint) is never transmitted to our servers. Authentication is handled entirely on your device.

## How We Use Your Information

- **Attendance Tracking**: To accurately record and report student attendance
- **AI Insights**: To generate personalized learning recommendations and engagement analysis
- **Notifications**: To send real-time attendance updates, alerts, and announcements
- **Platform Improvement**: To analyze usage patterns and improve the application
- **Security**: To protect against unauthorized access and ensure platform integrity

## Data Sharing

We **do not** sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

- **Your Institution**: Attendance records, engagement data, and academic insights are shared with your educational institution
- **Service Providers**: Encrypted cloud hosting providers (AWS) and push notification services (Firebase)
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or to protect rights and safety

## Data Security

- **Encryption**: All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Face Embeddings**: Encrypted with rotating keys, never shared, auto-deleted after 30 days
- **Authentication**: JWT tokens with automatic refresh, biometric authentication option
- **Access Control**: Role-based access control ensuring users only see authorized data

## Data Retention

- **Attendance Records**: Retained for the current academic year plus 1 year
- **Face Embeddings**: Automatically deleted after 30 days
- **Analytics Data**: Anonymized and aggregated, retained for 2 years
- **Account Data**: Retained until account deletion request

## Your Rights

- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your account and associated data
- **Export**: Request your data in a portable format
- **Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw consent for optional data collection

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@smartcampus-ai.com

## Children's Privacy

SmartCampus AI is designed for use in educational institutions. We collect data from students only with institutional consent and in compliance with FERPA regulations.

## International Compliance

- **GDPR**: Compliant for users in the European Union
- **FERPA**: Compliant for users in the United States
- **PIPEDA**: Compliant for users in Canada

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be notified through the app and via email.
